Questions

When do the socks ship?

We're testing samples from our manufacturers right now. The founding batch opens once a sample passes our 20-wash test, and ships about three weeks after that. Waitlist members hear first.

Why do I need grip socks for reformer Pilates?

The reformer carriage moves and the footbar is smooth. Grip socks stop you slipping and keep the equipment hygienic, which is why many studios ask you to wear them.

How does the Grip Guarantee work?

If the grip wears away in normal use within 90 days of delivery, send a photo of the sole and your order number. We send a replacement pair free.

How should I wash them?

Inside out, cold wash, no bleach, air dry. Skip the dryer, because heat is what peels grip off most socks.

Do you ship outside India?

Not yet. India first. Join the list and tell us your country if you'd like us to ship to you.
